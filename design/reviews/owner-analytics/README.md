# Owner analytics visual review

Reviewed 5 November 2026, at a 393 × 852 mobile viewport, with separate overflow checks at 320 and 430 px. Test figures and names are fixtures, never production defaults.

- `overview.png`: date presets, dominant revenue total, period comparison and haircut/time metrics.
- `daily-chart.png`: tap-selected exact value, positive revenue and a correction below zero, daily/weekly and revenue/haircut controls.
- `barber.png`: selecting a team member scrolls back to their scoped dashboard with counts, revenue, duration and published ratings.

Design reference: [Fresha's official Performance insights mobile screenshot](https://images.fresha.com/fresha-cms/filter_By_Location_mobile_1_2e43b1de02.jpg?f_quality=75&f_width=1920). BookMyTurn retains its approved cream/eucalyptus theme and existing typography. Reference imagery is not bundled into the app.
